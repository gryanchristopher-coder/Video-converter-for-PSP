with auto rotate to right.bat   -if there's a vertical video in the batch, it will rotate it to the right

with auto rotate to left.bat    -if there's a vertical video in the batch, it will rotate it to the left

Basic Convert.bat               -will convert the videos without rotating the vertical videos
